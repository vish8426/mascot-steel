## Overview

The proposal addresses the 'Grand Challenge' of the Climate Change Emergency by evaluating Mascot Steel's current operations and identifying opportunities for sustainable optimisation. Through detailed analysis and strategic planning, the project aims to guide the company towards environmentally responsible practices.

## Objectives

- Conduct a SWOT analysis to assess internal and external factors affecting Mascot Steel.
- Develop policy recommendations that support sustainable engineering practices.
- Critically review existing policies and suggest improvements aligned with CCE mitigation.
- Provide actionable insights for stakeholders to implement sustainable strategies.

## Key Deliverables

- **SWOT Analysis**: A detailed assessment of strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats related to Mascot Steel's operations.
- **Policy Recommendation**: A presentation outlining strategic policies for sustainable engineering practices.
- **Critical Review**: An in-depth analysis of current policies with suggestions for enhancement.
- **Engineering Optimisation Proposal**: A comprehensive document detailing proposed optimisations for Mascot Steel.

## Target Audience

- **Mascot Steel Management**: For internal strategy development and policy implementation.
- **Shareholders and Investors**: To understand the company's direction towards sustainability.
- **Policy Makers and Regulatory Bodies**: For insights into industry-specific sustainable practices.
- **Academic and Research Institutions**: As a case study for sustainable engineering optimisation.
